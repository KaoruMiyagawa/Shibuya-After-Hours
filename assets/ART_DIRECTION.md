# Billboard asset provenance

`after-hours.png` was generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool for this scene. No reference image was used. This is fictional original advertising artwork, not a real festival announcement.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: flat rectangular billboard texture for an original Tokyo nightlife 3D scene. Create a spectacular premium Japanese futuristic music festival advertisement, landscape 3:2 format, edge to edge graphic artwork without any billboard frame or environment. Closeup beautiful adult Japanese female futuristic fashion model, silver bob haircut, iridescent translucent visor and futuristic in-ear headphones, pale porcelain skin, one eye visible, ethereal but sharp photoreal fashion editorial. Face occupies right half, dark inky teal left side with huge elegant white headline '夜を、超えて。' and smaller English 'AFTER HOURS' and 'TOKYO / 2096'. Cyan and coral red rim lighting, rich black background, subtle halftone electronic graphics, tiny technical editorial typography and glowing orange disc behind head. Impeccable luxury art direction, highly detailed skin and reflective chrome, sophisticated contrast. No city, no buildings, no mockup, no extra borders. The graphic is directly applied to a giant LED screen.

All remaining billboard graphics, signs, windows and shop interiors are generated with Canvas 2D in `scene.js`. The main artwork is used as an actual texture on a 3D LED facade, rather than as a static scene background.
